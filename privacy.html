Who we are

This policy covers Blast SMS, a Tennessee-based texting program used to reach members of a team of about 100 people. Blast is the method: each person gets a private 1-to-1 text, not a group chat.

How you opt in

Consent is collected verbally at initial sign-up. By giving that verbal consent and providing a mobile number, you agree to receive SMS from this program. We do not buy lists or add numbers from public directories.

What we collect

  • Mobile phone number
  • Name and a team label we keep on the roster
  • SMS opt-in / opt-out status and the time of that choice
  • Message content we send or receive on the thread

How we use it

We use this information only to operate Blast: send team communications as private 1-to-1 texts, keep a roster, honor STOP and START, and keep a record of the thread. We do not use numbers for advertising to the public.

We do not share mobile numbers

We do not share or sell mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Carriers and our texting vendor (Twilio) process messages solely to deliver SMS. They are not given the roster to market to you.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You may get several texts in a busy week and none in others. This is not a daily marketing club. Typical traffic is operational notices and team communications for a group of about 100 people in Tennessee.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier’s plan governs any charges for sending or receiving SMS.

Opt out and opt back in

Reply STOP to the number that texted you to cancel. You will be removed from the thread. If you previously opted out and want back on, you must opt back in by text: reply START to that same number. We will not add you back from a verbal request alone after a STOP.

Reply HELP for help.

Retention

We keep roster and thread records as long as needed to run the program, then delete or de-identify them.

Contact

Questions: reply HELP to the program number, or contact Blast in Tennessee. See also the Terms and Conditions.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page. Continued use of the program after an update means you accept the revised policy, subject to STOP at any time.
